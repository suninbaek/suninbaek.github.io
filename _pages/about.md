## About
I am a graduate researcher at the [Movement Intelligence Laboratory](https://www.daekyumkim.com/home), advised by [Prof. Daekyum Kim](https://www.daekyumkim.com/members/professor). I am currently pursuing a M.S. degree at Korea University, where I received my B.S. degrees in Mechanical Engineering and Medical Convergence Engineering in 2025.


My research focuses on **advanced control of lower-limb wearable robots** using artificial intelligence and multimodal sensing. I study how wearable robots can adapt to humans in real-world conditions by integrating and interpreting diverse sensory inputs, including IMUs, vision, EMG, ECG, PPG, and FSR, as well as human feedback such as user preferences, to enable **adaptive, task-agnostic assistance** during daily locomotion.

More broadly, I am interested in **human–robot interaction** through wearable robotics, with a particular emphasis on how control strategies can move beyond predefined tasks toward seamless integration with human intent, state, and behavior.

My long-term vision is to develop wearable robots that not only assist or augment human mobility, but ultimately integrate with the human body as intuitive and responsive partners in everyday life.

Please feel free to explore my work and reach out for potential collaborations! (**[CV](https://suninbaek.github.io/cv/)**)
